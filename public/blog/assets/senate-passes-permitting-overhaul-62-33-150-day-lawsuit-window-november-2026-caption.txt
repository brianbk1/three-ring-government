62–33: the Senate just passed the biggest permitting overhaul in decades ⚡

The bill would set deadlines for environmental reviews and sharply shorten the time to sue over energy and infrastructure projects. Fifteen members of the Democratic caucus joined most Republicans; 31 Democrats and 2 Republicans voted no. Now it heads to the House before Democrats take over in January.

The data says…
⏱️ Environmental impact statements took 4.5 years on average from 2010–18 (CEQ). The bill sets a 2-year deadline.
⚖️ The window to sue over a review drops to 150 days, vs. a 6-year default under federal law.
🔌 2,061 GW of power projects were waiting to connect to the grid at the end of 2025 (LBNL).
🏗️ Only 13% of grid capacity requested from 2000–2020 was running by end of 2025; 75% withdrew (LBNL).
