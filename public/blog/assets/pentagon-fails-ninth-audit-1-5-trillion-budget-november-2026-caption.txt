=== DATA-FIRST ===
9 audits. 9 failures. Now the ask is $1.5 trillion. 📊

On Monday the Pentagon failed its ninth straight financial audit, and its inspector general issued a disclaimer of opinion. The CFO says more of the department passes each year. Critics on the left and right are pointing to the White House's $1.5 trillion defense request.

The data says…
💰 $4.9 trillion in assets and $5.2 trillion in liabilities were under audit (Breaking Defense)
✅ 12–14 of 25 reporting entities got clean opinions, up from 11 last year (Breaking Defense; Reuters)
📉 Material weaknesses went from 26 to 14, but the IG says that came from consolidation, not fixes
📅 The Pentagon says it will pass a full audit by 2028, the deadline Congress set
