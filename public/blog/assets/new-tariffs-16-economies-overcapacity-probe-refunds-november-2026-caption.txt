DATA-FIRST

New tariffs could hit 16 economies as soon as this week 📦

The White House is preparing tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies over industrial subsidies, The Wall Street Journal reports. It would rebuild much of what the Supreme Court struck down in February, under a different law. Final rates haven't been announced.

The data says…
💸 In June, the government refunded $49.2B in struck-down tariffs and collected $23.6B (Tax Foundation).
🌍 The EU, Japan and South Korea would reportedly face 15% or less; China the steepest rates (WSJ).
📈 The effective tariff rate hit 7.7% in 2025, the highest since 1947 (Tax Foundation).
🏠 Estimated average cost: about $820 per household in 2026 (Tax Foundation estimate).
