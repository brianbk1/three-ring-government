DATA-FIRST

🚢 The White House just proposed new tariffs on 14 economies, and the top rate isn't China's.

The U.S. Trade Representative says China, the EU, Japan and 11 others run industrial policies that create "structural excess capacity." The rates are proposals: comments are due Jan. 5 and a hearing is set for Jan. 25. Mexico and Norway, also investigated, got no determination yet.

The data says…
📊 Proposed ceilings run from 15% (EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan) to 35% (Vietnam). China's is 20%. (USTR)
📉 U.S. imports from China fell $130.4B in 2025, while Vietnam rose $57.3B and Taiwan $85.2B. (Census/BEA)
🇲🇽 Mexico, held back, ran a $196.9B U.S. goods deficit in 2025, third-largest of the 16 investigated. (Census/BEA)
🗳️ A Reuters/Ipsos poll found 62% would back repealing the tariffs, as reported by RTÉ's wire story.
