📺 A federal judge just froze the FCC's early review of all 8 ABC-owned TV station licenses.

Judge Loren AliKhan found ABC likely to win its claim that the review was First Amendment retaliation. The FCC says the review is about Disney's DEI practices and calls the ruling wrong on the facts and the law. The lawsuit continues.

The data says…
📅 The FCC order came 1 day after Trump posted that Jimmy Kimmel should be fired. (Variety)
⏩ The 8 licenses were called in 2.6 to 5.3 years before they were due, 4.3 years on average. (FCC schedule; our math)
🕰️ Reports say the FCC hadn't used early-renewal power in more than 50 years. (Reason)
⚖️ The opinion cites 11 Trump statements targeting ABC's licenses, per MeidasTouch's account.
