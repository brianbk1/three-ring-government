DATA-FIRST

🏛️ A federal planning panel just scheduled a New Year's Eve vote on Trump's D.C. projects, three days before Democrats take Congress.

The National Capital Planning Commission, which approves building in the capital, set a special Dec. 31 meeting that could give final approval to a rebuilt East Potomac golf course, a Tidal Basin redesign and painting the Eisenhower Executive Office Building white. Supporters say the projects replace aging facilities and honor the nation; critics call them costly vanity projects. In January, two of the panel's seats pass from Republicans to Democrats.

The data says…
🗳️ The panel approved Trump's 250-foot arch 8–4 on Nov. 5; all 4 D.C. members voted no (AP)
⚖️ The administration holds 6 of 12 seats, and a 6–6 tie can't approve a project (NCPC; Newsmax)
💵 Reported ballroom estimates rose from ~$200M to ~$600M; WaPo records show $293M private. The White House says it's privately funded (Axios; WaPo)
📊 65% of adults were negative on the ballroom, Reflecting Pool and arch in a July WaPo-Ipsos poll
