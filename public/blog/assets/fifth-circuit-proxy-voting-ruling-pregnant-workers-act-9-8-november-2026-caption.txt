9–8: a federal appeals court says the House broke the Constitution passing a $1.7 trillion bill in 2022 ⚖️

The full Fifth Circuit ruled Friday that members voting by proxy can't count toward a quorum. For now, the order only blocks the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act, passed in that bill, from being enforced against Texas as an employer. Advocates expect a Supreme Court appeal.

The data says…
🗳️ 226 of the 427 recorded House votes on Dec. 23, 2022, were cast by proxy (Fifth Circuit opinion).
🏛️ That left about 201 in person, under the 216 needed for a quorum (Down the Middle calc. from the House Clerk roll call).
✅ The bill still passed 225–201 (House Clerk).
🤰 Pregnant workers filed 1,869 accommodation charges with the EEOC in the law's first 11 months (Bloomberg Law).
